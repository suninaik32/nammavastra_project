NammaVastra

Empowering the Weavers of Karnataka through Digital Innovation

The Vision

NammaVastra is a dedicated ecosystem designed to bridge the gap between ancient looms and modern global fashion.

Heritage Preservation

Protecting the legacy of Ilkal and Molakalmuru silk weaving.

Digital Empowerment

Providing tools for weavers to manage their business independently.

Market Access

Direct connectivity between artisans and global fashionistas.

Fair Trade

Ensuring transparent pricing and sustainable livelihoods.

Solving Real Problems

The weaving industry faces critical roadblocks that we are dismantling:

  • ✕

    Middlemen Dependency: Weavers often lose up to 60% of their revenue to intermediaries.

  • ✕

    Costing Confusion: Manual calculations lead to underpricing and financial loss.

  • ✕

    Trend Lag: Traditional weavers often miss out on rapidly changing global fashion cycles.

Core Features

Our platform combines mobile agility with web-based enterprise management.

Smart Calculator

Factor in silk weight, zari cost, and labor automatically.

Digital Gallery

Manage high-resolution portfolios on-the-go.

Trendboard

Get AI-curated insights into colors and patterns trending globally.

Inventory Sync

Real-time updates between mobile apps and web dashboards.
